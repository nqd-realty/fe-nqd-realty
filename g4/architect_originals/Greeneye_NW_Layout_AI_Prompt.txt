NW STRIP — OVERALL DIMENSIONS
──────────────────────────────
The NW construction strip runs along the West boundary at the North end of plot.

Total N-S length    : 30ft (from North boundary going South)
  └─ Car parking    : 15ft (North end — touching North boundary)
  └─ Construction   : 15ft (South of parking)

Total E-W width     : 14ft (from West boundary going East)
  └─ 5ft setback    : Mandatory road setback from West boundary (NO construction)
  └─ 9ft usable     : Actual buildable width after setback

Maximum height      : 10ft (single storey only)


SETBACK PATHWAY — KEY DESIGN FEATURE
──────────────────────────────────────
The 5ft setback strip (between West road boundary and buildable wall) is used
as an OPEN WALKING PATHWAY. Not roofed or enclosed — fully open to sky.

- South end  : Opens toward nursery land / South road direction (open, no gate)
- North end  : Opens into the car parking area (open, no gate or wall)
- Runs the full 15ft construction height (N-S)
- Serves as the ONLY access route to the common toilet
- No construction, no roof, no obstruction in this 5ft strip at any point

TOILET VENTILATION — DETAILED SPECIFICATION
─────────────────────────────────────────────
System        : Cross ventilation — mechanical exhaust + natural inlet

EXHAUST (air OUT):
  Wall        : West wall of toilet
  Type        : Exhaust fan with grille cover
  Height      : ~7ft from floor (above door height, above head level)
  Opening     : Approx 1ft wide x 1.5ft high
  Discharge   : Into 5ft open setback pathway → open sky at both ends
  Vastu       : West = Varuna (water deity) — correct for waste/exhaust

INLET (fresh air IN):
  Wall        : East wall of toilet
  Type        : Fixed high louvre window (horizontal slats, fixed angle)
  Height      : ~7ft from floor (same height as exhaust — maximises flow)
  Opening     : Approx 2ft wide x 1.5ft high
  Source      : Open nursery land — consistent fresh air availability
  Privacy     : Above head height — zero privacy concern
  Vastu       : East = Surya (Sun) — fresh, purifying, life-giving energy

Air flow path : Fresh air IN (East louvre) → across 9ft → Exhaust OUT (West fan)
                Mechanical fan ensures active circulation even on still days


ROOM INTERIOR LAYOUT (9ft x 9ft)
──────────────────────────────────
1. SLAB / UTILITY COUNTER
   - Position  : North wall, full 7.5ft usable width, 1ft deep
   - Material  : RCC with smooth plaster finish
   - Height    : ~2.5ft from floor (standard counter height)
   - Use       : Utility surface — water vessel, belongings, small items

2. STORAGE UNIT
   - Position  : West wall, floor to ceiling, 1.5ft deep
   - Type      : Built-in shelved cupboard (3-4 shelves)
   - Use       : Caretaker belongings + nursery tools
   - Vastu     : Weight on West wall — correct

3. BED PLACEMENT
   - Position    : South-central portion of room
   - Orientation : Head to South (aligns with Earth's magnetic field)
   - East wall   : Kept clear for door access and morning light

4. NE SACRED CORNER
   - Position : North-East corner (between slab end and East wall)
   - Use      : Small lamp or potted plant ONLY — nothing heavy

5. NO COOKING FACILITY
   - Phase 1 is rest/sleep only
   - No cooking, no stove, no gas point in this phase


DOOR SCHEDULE
──────────────
Door 1 — TOILET WEST DOOR
  Location  : West wall of toilet — South-biased position
  Position  : ~0.5ft jamb from South (dividing) wall
              Door occupies South 3ft of West wall
              4ft+ clearance to NW sub-corner (Vayu zone free)
  Width     : ~2.5ft clear opening
  Hinge     : South jamb — door swings inward toward East wall
              (away from WC, away from NW corner)
  Swing     : Inward into toilet, toward East/basin side
  Access    : From 5ft setback pathway only
  Users     : All users — only door to toilet

              Aligns with primary approach from South pathway entry
              Door swing clears away from WC and NW corner

  Location  : East wall of caretaker room, mid-wall position
  Width     : ~3ft clear
  Swing     : Inward into room
  Access    : From open nursery land only
  Users     : Caretaker only — only door to room

CONSTRUCTION SPECIFICATIONS
─────────────────────────────
Wall material   : Brick masonry, minimum 4.5 inch (one brick) thickness
Roof type       : Flat RCC slab (preferred) or mild sloped — max 10ft height
Flooring        : IPS or Kota stone
Wall finish     : Light green or cream paint, interior and exterior
Plumbing        : All pipes along West wall inside 5ft setback zone
Drainage        : WEST DIRECT EXIT — see full spec in UNIT 1 section above
Ventilation     : Toilet — exhaust fan West wall + louvre window East wall
                  Both openings at ~7ft height (above door/head level)
                  Room — East door is primary ventilation source
                  NO North wall window (North wall = solid shared wall with
                  toilet — any opening would pull toilet air into room)
                  East wall high louvre above door if extra vent needed
Electrical      : One light + one fan point per unit minimum
                  One exhaust fan power point on West wall of toilet

Why South Wall Works Best for the Urinal

9ft of space: The South wall runs 9ft E-W — far more room than the 6ft West wall or 6ft North wall. After reserving ~3.2ft for the door swing zone (SW corner) and ~3.2ft for the shower (SE corner), there's a clean ~2.6ft in the center for the urinal with comfortable gaps on both sides.

Natural flow: Walk in through the West door → urinal is immediately to your right on the South wall. Quick-access use case (urinal visitors) doesn't require walking deep into the room.

Privacy separation: WC is in the NW corner (opposite end of room from urinal in S-center). No partition wall needed — they're naturally separated by distance and angle.

East wall freed up: The entire East wall is now clean for the high louvre ventilation window, geyser, and clear air flow path for cross-ventilation (E→W).

No Vastu conflict: The South wall is Yama's wall — solid and closed. Mounting a urinal on it doesn't create any opening. The wall remains structurally solid between toilet and caretaker room.

Plumbing efficiency: Urinal drain (2") drops straight down into the South wall base, short horizontal run to join the main soil pipe that exits West. Minimal pipe length.

Specifications & Notes — Final v3
All fixtures · Plumbing · Vastu · Construction
Urinal — South Wall (center) Wall-hung ceramic urinal with concealed flush valve. Positioned ~3.2ft from W end (clear of door swing) and ~1ft gap before shower zone. User faces South. Cold water supply drops from N wall main line through concealed conduit. 2" drain drops straight into South wall base, short run to join main soil pipe. Brands: Hindware Flat Back, Parryware Luco. Flush: sensor or push-type.
WC — NW Zone (unchanged) Floor-mounted western commode, cistern against N wall, user faces East (Vastu ✓). Health faucet on right. 4" soil pipe runs to main drain along S wall base, exits West. NW zone is correct for toilet per Vastu (Vayu energy). Brands: Hindware, Parryware, Cera.
Handwash Counter — NE Corner 24"×18" granite or Kota stone counter against N wall (East portion). Table-top or under-mount basin. Single-lever mixer with hot + cold. Mirror on N wall above. Position matches architect's original plan. Now has full N-wall width to spread out without competing with urinal on E wall.
Shower — SE Corner ~3ft × 2.5ft zone. 8" rain shower + handheld unit. Mixer connected to geyser. Glass partition (6mm tempered) on West edge separates wet/dry zones. Dedicated 3" floor drain. Anti-skid tiles. Floor slope 1:40 to drain.
Geyser — East Wall 15L storage geyser wall-mounted at ~6ft height. Cold inlet from N wall main. Hot outlets to handwash mixer and shower mixer. CPVC green hot water piping. 2kW ISI. Separate 15A socket + MCB on E wall. Brands: Bajaj, Racold, AO Smith, Havells.
Door — West Wall (unchanged) South-biased: 0.5ft from S wall, 2.5ft wide. Occupies South 3ft of West wall. Hinged S jamb, swings inward toward E. NW Vayu sub-corner 4ft+ free. West wall now has ONLY the door + exhaust fan — no fixture conflicts.
User Flow (improved) Enter W door → urinal immediately to right (S wall, quick access) → WC diagonally ahead-left (NW) → handwash ahead-right (NE) → shower far-right (SE). Quick-use visitors (urinal only) don't need to cross the room. Full-use visitors flow naturally NW→NE→SE.
Plumbing Advantage Urinal on S wall means its 2" drain drops directly into the South wall base — zero horizontal run. The main drain collector runs along the S wall base from E to W, collecting: urinal (S-center), basin (NE), shower (SE), floor drain (center), and WC soil (NW). Everything converges and exits West. Very efficient gravity drainage.
Vastu — No Conflict South wall (Yama) must remain solid — no opening. Mounting a urinal on the interior face doesn't create any opening in the dividing wall. The wall's structural integrity is preserved. Urinal plumbing is all on the toilet side. Caretaker room side remains clean and untouched.
Construction Notes Urinal backing requires a 4.5" brick wall minimum (already specified). Concealed flush valve housing needs a 6"×6" recess in wall — plan during masonry, not after. Waterproofing on S wall behind urinal up to 4ft height. All pipe chases to be planned before plastering.

Design Specifications & Notes
Fixtures · Plumbing · Vastu · Construction — Jaipur, Rajasthan
WC — Western Commode Floor-mounted western commode with concealed cistern, positioned in NW zone of toilet (Vastu correct). User faces East (classical Vastu requirement). Health faucet on right side. 4" PVC SWR soil pipe exits via West wall. Brands: Hindware, Parryware, Cera.
Urinal Wall-hung ceramic urinal with concealed flush valve, positioned on West wall south of WC. Separated from WC by a 3.5ft half-wall partition for privacy. 2" drain connects to main soil line. Quick access from the West door. Brands: Hindware Flat Back, Parryware.
Handwash Counter 24"×18" granite or Kota stone counter in NE corner (basin location per original architect plan). Under-mounted or table-top ceramic basin. Single-lever mixer tap with hot (from geyser) + cold connection. Mirror on East wall above.
Shower Zone ~3ft × 2.5ft shower area in SE corner with 8" overhead rain shower + wall-mounted handheld unit. Thermostatic or single-lever mixer connected to geyser hot + cold. 6mm tempered glass partition separates wet/dry zones. Dedicated 3" floor drain. Anti-skid tiles mandatory.
Geyser — 15L Storage Wall-mounted at ~6ft height on East wall, between handwash and shower zones. Cold water inlet from main supply line running along North wall. Hot water outlet branches to: (1) handwash mixer, (2) shower mixer. CPVC hot water pipes (green). Brands: Bajaj, Racold, AO Smith, Havells. 2kW element, ISI marked.
Ventilation Cross-ventilation: Exhaust fan on West wall @7ft (air exits to open setback pathway), high louvre window on East wall @7ft (fresh Surya air from nursery land). Air flows E→W across full 9ft width. No north vent (parking wall), no south vent (solid dividing wall).
Drainage — West Direct Exit All drains converge to main 4" soil pipe exiting West wall. Trench runs under 5ft setback (~1:40 gradient). Inspection chamber at West boundary edge. Soak pit just outside boundary (road side). Capped stub for future municipal sewer connection.
Vastu Compliance Toilet in NW zone (Vayu — correct for water/waste). WC faces East. West door south-biased (NW Vayu sub-corner 4ft+ free). Drain exits West (Varuna). Exhaust West, fresh air East. Basin in NE corner. All plumbing runs along West wall zone. 95% Vastu compliant per architect brief.
Waterproofing Dr. Fixit or Pidilite membrane on entire floor and walls up to 5ft. Shower zone waterproofed to full ceiling height. Floor slope 1:40 towards floor drains. IPS or anti-skid Kota stone flooring. Shower zone separated by glass partition to keep dry zone dry.
Electrical Points 1× ceiling light (LED 12W), 1× exhaust fan point (West wall @7ft), 1× geyser power point (East wall @6ft, 15A socket with MCB), 1× switch board near door (West wall). All wiring in PVC conduit. Separate MCB for geyser. RCCB protection mandatory for wet zone.

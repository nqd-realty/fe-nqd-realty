The Collision Problem

The West wall is only 6ft long (N-S). Per the architect's door schedule:

• Door is south-biased — 0.5ft from South (dividing) wall, 2.5ft wide

• Door occupies the South 3ft of the West wall (from 3ft to 6ft from North)

• The North 4ft+ must stay free for the NW Vayu sub-corner (Vastu)


Result: The door takes 3ft, Vayu zone takes 4ft → that's 7ft on a 6ft wall. They overlap by design (the door starts inside the Vayu clearance zone). There is literally ZERO remaining West wall space for a urinal.


Fix: Move the urinal to the East wall, between the handwash counter (NE corner) and the shower zone (SE corner). This keeps the West wall clean for the door, preserves Vayu corner, and creates a logical flow: enter → urinal straight ahead → WC to the left.

Key Change: Urinal Placement Moved from West wall → East wall (mid-section, between handwash NE and shower SE). The West wall has only 6ft N-S: the door takes the South 3ft, and the NW Vayu zone requires 4ft+ clearance from the North corner. No room for a urinal. On the East wall, the urinal gets its own dedicated space, has direct cold water from the main line, and creates a natural user flow: enter door → urinal straight ahead.
WC — Western Commode NW zone of toilet, cistern against North wall, user faces East (Vastu ✓). Floor-mounted with concealed cistern. Health faucet on right. 4" PVC SWR soil pipe runs along South wall, exits West. Brands: Hindware, Parryware, Cera.
Urinal — East Wall Wall-hung ceramic urinal with concealed flush valve. Position: East wall mid-section, between handwash counter (NE) and shower (SE). Cold water supply drops from main line running along N wall. 2" drain runs along floor to join main soil pipe. Privacy: naturally screened by distance from WC. Brands: Hindware Flat Back, Parryware.
Handwash Counter — NE Corner 24"×18" granite/Kota stone counter. Under-mount or table-top ceramic basin. Single-lever mixer (hot from geyser + cold). Mirror on East wall above counter. Position matches original architect plan (basin in NE corner).
Shower — SE Corner ~3.2ft × 1.8ft shower zone. 8" overhead rain shower + handheld unit. Thermostatic or single-lever mixer connected to geyser. 6mm tempered glass partition on West side separates wet/dry zones. Dedicated 3" floor drain. Anti-skid tiles. Floor slope 1:40.
Geyser — 15L, East Wall Wall-mounted at ~6ft height on East wall, above urinal area. Cold inlet from N wall main line. Hot outlet branches to: handwash mixer + shower mixer. CPVC hot water pipes. 2kW ISI element. Brands: Bajaj, Racold, AO Smith. Separate 15A socket + MCB.
Door — West Wall (unchanged) South-biased: 0.5ft from S dividing wall, 2.5ft wide, occupies South 3ft of West wall. Hinged on South jamb, swings inward toward East. 4ft+ NW Vayu sub-corner kept free. Accessed via 5ft setback pathway.
User Flow Enter West door → dry movement corridor in center → urinal straight ahead (East wall) → WC to the left (NW) → handwash in NE corner → shower in SE corner. Wet zone (shower) isolated by glass partition. Dry zone maintained for all movement paths.
Ventilation (unchanged) Exhaust fan: West wall @7ft (air OUT to setback pathway). High louvre: East wall @7ft (fresh air IN from nursery). Cross-ventilation E→W across 9ft. No N/S vents (solid walls).
Drainage (unchanged route) All drains converge to 4" main soil pipe along South wall base, exits West wall. Under 5ft setback trench (1:40 gradient). Inspection chamber at W boundary. Soak pit outside. Capped stub for future municipal sewer.

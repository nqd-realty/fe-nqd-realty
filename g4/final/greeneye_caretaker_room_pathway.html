L-Shaped Utility Counter — NW Corner (1.5ft deep) RCC with smooth plaster finish, L-shaped wrapping the NW corner. North arm: 5ft along the North wall. West arm: ~3.4ft down the West wall, ending where the storage cupboard begins. Both arms are **1.5ft deep** at 2.5ft height — deep enough for a kettle, prep area, vessels, phone charging, and daily items. The extra half-foot of depth over standard 1ft counters makes it genuinely usable as a workstation — you can place items two-deep without crowding the edge.
Storage Cupboard — SW Corner (5.5ft) Built-in shelved cupboard on the West wall, SW portion. 1.5ft deep, 5.5ft long (starts ~3.5ft from North wall, runs to South wall). 4 shelves — generous storage for caretaker belongings, nursery tools, and supplies. Vastu: weight on West + South walls = double benefit. NW corner stays completely open for counter access. Bed sits immediately East of the cupboard.
Bed — 6ft × 6ft (King), South Zone Square king bed filling the South zone. Head faces South (Vastu ✓). The bed's North edge aligns with the door zone (~3.5ft from N wall), leaving the North half of the room as open living space. ~0.5ft gap on West for storage access, ~1ft gap on East for louver airflow circulation. The bed receives direct cross-ventilation from the SE louver and morning Surya light through the slats.
East Door — NE Corner, Hinged N, Swings Along N Wall Door moved to the NE corner of the East wall. 3ft wide, hinged on the North jamb. Swings inward flat against the North wall — completely out of the way when open. Creates a clear, unobstructed entry: walk in from nursery land → the entire room opens up to the left (counter, storage) and ahead (open floor, bed). No collision with any furniture. Switch board placed right inside the door on the left. Surya direction ✓.
Louver Window — East Wall (~6ft, South of Door) With the door at the NE corner, the louver window now spans a massive ~6ft of the East wall (from door's south jamb all the way to the SE corner). Double the original 3ft. This dramatically improves both ventilation and natural light — the entire bed zone and South half of the room is bathed in Surya morning light and fresh air. Operable aluminum slats for control.
L-Counter — NW Corner (stepped depth) RCC, 2.5ft height. North arm: ~6.3ft, 1ft deep — shelf for vessel, phone, mirror, daily items. West arm: ~3.4ft, 1.5ft deep (flush with storage) — houses the induction cooktop at the top and prep/utensil area below. The stepped depth gives the North arm a slim profile while the West arm has enough surface for cooking.
Kitchenette — West Arm (hotel-style pantry) Five-star room style mini-kitchen on the West arm counter. **Under counter:** 50L mini refrigerator (milk, eggs, butter, cold drinks, leftovers). **On counter:** single-burner portable induction plate for tea, coffee, omelette, boiled eggs, milk boiling. **North arm (nearby):** electric kettle, cups, sugar, coffee/tea sachets. Dedicated 15A socket with MCB on the West wall. The high louvre @7ft directly above handles steam from boiling. No LPG, no open flame — fully electric. The 1.5ft counter depth fits a standard induction plate (~1ft) with space for a small chopping board behind.
Louver Window — East Wall (South of Door) Replaces the fixed glass panel. ~3ft wide × full wall height, from door's south jamb to SE corner. Operable horizontal aluminum louver slats — caretaker can angle them open for maximum airflow (night sleeping) or closed for rain/dust protection. Even when closed, slats allow partial airflow through gaps. Faces open nursery land — no privacy concern. Still brings in Surya morning light through the slat angles. Weatherproof aluminum frame with silicone seal. Plan the opening during masonry, not after.
High Louvre — West Wall @7ft (Air OUT) Fixed horizontal louvre above the L-counter's West arm, at ~7ft height (~1ft wide × 1.5ft high). Slats angled to prevent rain ingress while allowing continuous air exhaust. Discharges into the 5ft open pathway (open to sky at both ends — stale air dissipates immediately). Position above head height so the outflow doesn't create a direct draft on anyone standing at the counter. Plan the opening during masonry. Vastu: West = Varuna direction, correct for air/waste exit.
